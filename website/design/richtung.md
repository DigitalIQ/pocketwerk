# Design direction (phase 4)

Mode: Persuade. Concept seed key: f3fcbd67 (scope direction, assigned index 3).

## Rut kept out of the list

The page this category always ships: dark gradient, floating glass screenshot, purple-blue glow, a grid of equal feature cards. Its predictable opposite: a stark white Apple-clone page. Neither is on the list.

## Seven grounded candidates, ordered by resonance

1. Letterpress type case: a drawer of compartments where every sort has its place (print and wood).
2. Workwear pocket: stitched denim pocket, rivets, contrast thread (textile; the literal reading of the name, counted once).
3. **Workshop pegboard and shadow board: painted perforated board, every tool hangs on its pegs inside its own outline (metal and paint).** Assigned by the roll.
4. Colour swatch fan and specimen book for the Design Studio (print and colour).
5. Split-flap departure board for switching Dock profiles (signage).
6. Japanese tool box with fitted compartments (lacquered wood).
7. Kiss-cut sticker sheet with app icons as stickers (print and vinyl).

## Challengers and verdicts

- Hand-processed 16 mm film: declined (no link to the Dock, hides the offer). Kept: nothing beyond frame-exact motion discipline.
- Bioluminescent night sea: declined (dark glow is the category rut). Kept: pointer-reactive motion as a real mechanism, matching the product's pointer-reactive backgrounds.
- Algorave code floor: declined (neon on black, monospace costume). Kept: "upcoming change visible before it happens", used for the profile switch preview.
- Darkroom safelight: declined (amber monochrome hides the product's colour themes).
- Ligne claire album: declined, but raises the direction: every illustration uses one unvarying line weight with flat fills and no cast shadows on drawings.
- Airport yellow wayfinding: declined, but raises the direction: one saturated field owns whole regions at full commitment, and numerals go monumental only where they carry information (⌘1 to ⌘9).

Decision 2026-10-13 (autonomous, see WEBSITE-STATUS.md): build the assigned pegboard direction with the two raises. No image generation is available, so the build is code-led; the ambition lives in the contract below.

## Contract

THESIS: Pocketwerk is a workshop wall for the Dock: every app has its place and every pocket hangs where you reach for it. Refuses the dark glass gradient hero with a floating screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: painted board green fields with a perforation grid, orange pockets as the only accent, paper-white tiles, one even 2 px line for all drawings, Archivo expanded for display, keycaps for keys, glass only where the product itself is glass (the Dock and the popups).

STORY: see the pocket mechanism, see a pocket at work, see how it can look, learn Dock profiles and the keyboard, trust the privacy, install past "Open Anyway", leave with the source one click away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed board green with holes. Left: H1 "Pockets for your Dock." at display scale, subtext, orange "Download for Mac" button and the Homebrew command with Copy. Right and bottom: a three.js glass Dock shelf with icons; pockets hang on pegs above three of them, sway with the pointer, the nearest pops open with a 200 ms rise. Poster image first.

FORM: workshop pegboard and shadow board, position 3 of 7, seed key f3fcbd67. Signature interaction: pockets swinging on pegs and popping open above the icon under the pointer. Motion grammar: the product's own open animation (fade with 8 pt rise, 200 ms) for every reveal.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
