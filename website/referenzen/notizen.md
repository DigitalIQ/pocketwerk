# Design references

Autonomous run: no favourite sites from a person, refero.design skipped (needs a visible Chrome session and a person to pick). References were chosen from the product's own world instead.

## A. Workshop pegboard and shadow board (chosen ground)

- Painted perforated board with a regular hole grid; every tool hangs on its pegs inside a painted outline of itself.
- Taken: the hole grid as the page's texture on the "board" sections, hanging pockets as the hero objects, outlines with one even line weight for illustrations, the feeling that everything has its place.
- Not taken: wood hardboard brown, rust, grunge, stencil lettering, hazard stripes.

## B. The macOS 26 Dock and its popups (the product itself)

- Glassy Dock shelf, app tiles on the standard icon grid, popups that rise from one icon.
- Taken: the Dock as the stage of the hero (rendered in 3D), the 200 ms rise with 8 pt lift as the page's motion, the real popup layouts (grid, list, tiles) as live components.
- Not taken: wallpaper photos, dark glow gradients, fake window chrome.

## C. Equipment plates and industrial labels

- Wide grotesque lettering stamped on machines and bins, numerals at large size where they carry information.
- Taken: Archivo in its expanded width for display, large keycaps for ⌘1-9.
- Not taken: monospace as decoration (mono only for the real Homebrew command).
