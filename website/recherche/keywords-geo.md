## What the result pages show

- The built-in answer is a Finder folder of app aliases dropped on the right side of the Dock as a stack. Forum users complain that it lives on the right side only and launched apps still get their own Dock icon.
- The paid utilities in this space are sold as one-time purchases or subscriptions. Free and open-source options exist but are few.
- One guide claims Launchpad was removed in macOS 26. Single source, unverified: not used on the site.
