Pocketwerk 1.0.0 is the first public release. It puts groups of apps, files, folders and links into the Mac Dock. Each group can get its own Dock icon, and a click opens it right above that icon.

## Highlights

- **Pockets in your Dock.** Give any group its own Dock icon. Click once to open the popup above it, click again to close it. One Dock icon can hold several groups; swipe between them.
- **A popup that works from the keyboard.** Grid, list or tiles. Arrow keys and Return, Space for Quick Look, type to search, ⌘1 to ⌘9 to jump between groups. Folders open inside the popup.
- **Whole groups at once.** Open All, Hide All and Quit All. ⌥-click quits one app, ⌘-click picks several items to open together.
- **Design Studio.** Liquid Glass, colors, gradients, photos, your blurred desktop picture, textures and animated backgrounds that react to the pointer. Dozens of built-in themes and presets, and your own.
- **Dock icons to match.** A mini grid of the apps inside, a fanned stack, a folder, an SF Symbol, an emoji, text or your own image.
- **Dock profiles.** Save the whole Dock and switch with a hotkey, the menu bar, the command line or a URL. Pocketwerk can quit and launch apps as it switches.
- **Smart groups.** Most Used, Recently Opened and Running Apps fill themselves.
- **Hotkeys without extra permissions.** Global hotkeys for groups, Dock icons and profiles, plus ⌥⇧1 to ⌥⇧9 out of the box.
- **Private.** No account, no tracking, no analytics. Data stays in one JSON file on your Mac. Network only for a daily update check and link favicons.
- English and German.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/DigitalIQ/pocketwerk/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

Requires macOS 26 Tahoe or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.

- Download `Pocketwerk-1.0.0.dmg` below, open it and drag Pocketwerk into Applications.
- Or use Homebrew: `brew install --cask digitaliq/tap/pocketwerk`

## First launch

Pocketwerk is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch once.

1. Open Pocketwerk and click **Done** in the warning.
2. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll down to **Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Pocketwerk.
4. Click **Open Anyway** again and confirm with your password or Touch ID.

After that it opens like any other app.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized.** The first launch needs "Open Anyway" as described above.
- **No Share extension.** To add a link or file from another app, select it and use **Services > Add to Pocketwerk**.
- **Spring-loading on Dock tiles** (opening a popup while you hover a drag over its Dock icon) comes in 1.1. Dropping files on a tile already adds them to its group.
- **Shortcuts discovery** through App Intents comes in 1.1. Until then, Shortcuts can drive Pocketwerk with its `pocketwerk://` URLs (see the [README](https://github.com/DigitalIQ/pocketwerk#automation)).
- Some utilities quit apps that have no open windows. Add Pocketwerk to their exceptions, or its Dock icons and hotkeys stop working.
