Pocketwerk 1.2.0 brings a Liquid Glass app icon, a calmer Dock Icons page and a handful of fixes.

## Highlights

- **Liquid Glass app icon.** The icon now follows your Mac: light, dark, tinted and clear. The Honey, Ocean, Graphite and Iris variants are glass too and keep their colors.
- **Dock Icons page.** Slots sit in a grouped list like the settings pages. Each slot's **…** menu has **Choose Groups…**, **Move Up** and **Move Down**.
- **Profile previews.** Every profile in the list shows its first Dock tiles.

## Fixes

- Opening the main window no longer selects the group name.
- Safari and other system apps show `/Applications` as their path.
- The Record Shortcut buttons use the normal font.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/DigitalIQ/pocketwerk/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Upgrading

Install the new version over the old one (download the DMG, or run `brew upgrade --cask pocketwerk`). Your groups, Dock icons and profiles stay.

## Install

Requires macOS 26 Tahoe or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.

- Download `Pocketwerk-1.2.0.dmg` below, open it and drag Pocketwerk into Applications.
- Or use Homebrew: `brew install --cask digitaliq/tap/pocketwerk`

## First launch

Pocketwerk is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch of a new download once.

1. Open Pocketwerk and click **Done** in the warning.
2. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll down to **Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Pocketwerk.
4. Click **Open Anyway** again and confirm with your password or Touch ID.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized.** The first launch needs "Open Anyway" as described above.
- **No Share extension.** To add a link or file from another app, select it and use **Services > Add to Pocketwerk**.
- **Leader key.** While Pocketwerk waits for the letter after the leader key, it briefly reserves the plain letter keys, so typing in other apps pauses for that moment.
- **Shortcuts discovery depends on macOS indexing.** The actions appear once macOS has indexed Pocketwerk. Keep the app in `/Applications` or `~/Applications` and open it once; if they are still missing, relaunch Pocketwerk or log out and in. The `pocketwerk://` URLs always work with the "Open URLs" action.
- **Assistant and model-based suggestions** need a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.
- **Profiles per Space** read the current Space through an undocumented macOS interface. If it is unavailable, profiles can still follow displays.
- Some utilities quit apps that have no open windows. Add Pocketwerk to their exceptions, or its Dock icons and hotkeys stop working.
