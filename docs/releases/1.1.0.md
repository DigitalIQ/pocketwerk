Pocketwerk 1.1.0 adds an on-device assistant, Dock profiles that follow your Spaces, Shortcuts and Siri actions, and many ways to reach a group faster.

## Highlights

- **Assistant.** A new tab in every group changes it from a plain sentence, such as "make it dark blue with a list view" or "add my music apps". It runs on your Mac with Apple Intelligence. Every change can be undone, and nothing is removed without your OK.
- **Suggestions.** The App Drawer suggests apps that fit a group, and **Suggested Groups** proposes groups from apps you use together. Without Apple Intelligence the suggestions come from app categories and your usage on this Mac.
- **Sidebar in the popup.** Choose a list of groups on the left instead of swiping, with ⌘ numbers and drop targets per group.
- **Faster in the popup.** Keys 1 to 9 open items on the current page; hold ⌃ to see the numbers. Missing items get **Locate…**.
- **Spring-loading.** Hold a drag over a Pocketwerk Dock icon and its popup opens, so you can drop exactly where you want.
- **Profiles per Space and display.** Link a profile to a Space or a display and Pocketwerk switches the Dock when you move there.
- **Richer profiles.** Open a project with an app or a browser in a given profile when a profile is applied. Leave a "where I left off" note when switching away. Cycle with Next Profile, keep profiles private, export and share the rest.
- **Shortcuts, Siri and Focus.** Actions to show a group, open, hide or quit its apps, and apply a profile or the next one. A Focus can apply a Dock profile.
- **Leader key, key sequences and a hot corner.** Press the leader key and a letter, set hotkeys to double or triple presses or follow-up keys, or rest the pointer in a screen corner to show all groups. Still no Accessibility permission.
- **A livelier Dock icon.** Pocketwerk's own Dock icon shows a live mini grid of your first group. The app icon comes in five color variants, and the menu bar icon in four styles.
- **More looks.** Glow and depth for popups, falling snow over textures and ready-made gradient swatches. Seasonal touches are available and off by default.
- **Help built in.** A searchable help window (⌘?), Report a Problem, and a Diagnostics page that exports a local log file.
- **Lighter animations.** Animated backgrounds stop when hidden or covered and run at most 30 frames per second on battery.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/DigitalIQ/pocketwerk/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Upgrading from 1.0

Install the new version over the old one (download the DMG, or run `brew upgrade --cask pocketwerk`). Your groups, Dock icons and profiles stay. The Dock launchers use a new format, so Pocketwerk rebuilds them once automatically on the first start. The Dock may restart once while that happens.

## Install

Requires macOS 26 Tahoe or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.

- Download `Pocketwerk-1.1.0.dmg` below, open it and drag Pocketwerk into Applications.
- Or use Homebrew: `brew install --cask digitaliq/tap/pocketwerk`

## First launch

Pocketwerk is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch of a new download once.

1. Open Pocketwerk and click **Done** in the warning.
2. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll down to **Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Pocketwerk.
4. Click **Open Anyway** again and confirm with your password or Touch ID.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized.** The first launch needs "Open Anyway" as described above.
- **No Share extension.** To add a link or file from another app, select it and use **Services > Add to Pocketwerk**.
- **Leader key.** While Pocketwerk waits for the letter after the leader key, it briefly reserves the plain letter keys, so typing in other apps pauses for that moment.
- **Shortcuts discovery depends on macOS indexing.** The actions appear once macOS has indexed Pocketwerk. Keep the app in `/Applications` or `~/Applications` and open it once; if they are still missing, relaunch Pocketwerk or log out and in. The `pocketwerk://` URLs always work with the "Open URLs" action.
- **Assistant and model-based suggestions** need a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.
- **Profiles per Space** read the current Space through an undocumented macOS interface. If it is unavailable, profiles can still follow displays.
- Some utilities quit apps that have no open windows. Add Pocketwerk to their exceptions, or its Dock icons and hotkeys stop working.
